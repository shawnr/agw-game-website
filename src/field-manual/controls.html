    Controls

    Default mouse and keyboard controls during an operation. Keyboard bindings can be changed under Settings → Key Bindings. Press / in-game for a shortcuts panel.

    Mouse

    ActionDoes
    Left-click a source or componentStart drawing a conduit from it
    Left-click a destinationFinish and build the conduit
    Left-click open ground while drawingPin a waypoint to steer the route
    Right-click while drawingRemove the last waypoint; with none left, cancel the line
    Right-click (idle)Open the radial menu
    Double right-clickDrop whatever tool you're holding and return to idle
    Shift + click a TransformerCycle its output type
    Shift + click a FilterCycle which type it passes (mixed sources)
    Click a reacting hexPin the interaction inspector
    Click a notificationPan to the building it mentions
    Right-drag or middle-dragPan the map
    Mouse wheelZoom in and out
    Hover over anythingTooltip with supply, demand, flow, loss and status

    Radial menu

    Right-click to open a ring of options around the cursor. What you see depends on what's under it:

    • Anywhere: Field Manual, Build (a sub-menu of unlocked components with prices), Demolish.
    • On a component: Remove, plus Cycle on a Transformer or Filter.
    • On a conduit: Tear Down and Upgrade (once upgrades are unlocked). Upgrade shows its cost.
    • On a building: Inspect.

    With the keyboard: arrows or WASD to choose a slice, Enter or E to select, Esc to back out.

    In-game Field Manual

    The in-game Field Manual has three tabs: Interactions, Components and Energy Types. Open it with C, from the radial menu, or from the Field Manual icon on your terminal.

    • Tab or ← → switches tabs.
    • ↑ ↓ or the mouse wheel scrolls.
    • Esc or C closes it.

    Pause menu

    Press Esc with nothing else to cancel: Resume, Save Game, Restart Operation, Settings, Leaderboards, Achievements, Minimize to Desktop, Quit Operation.
