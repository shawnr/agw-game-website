    Energy Types

    The realm runs on five kinds of magickal energy, measured in mana.

    How the types differ

    All five types follow the same rules on the grid: the same conduit capacity (15 mana per day, or 30 upgraded), the same dissipation (0.2 mana per hex), the same costs. What makes each one different is:

    • Who needs it and where it comes from on a given map.
    • What it reacts with when conduits of two types share or neighbor a hex. See Interactions.
    • How it converts in a Transformer. Some conversions are cheap, some are wasteful, and some aren't possible at all.

    Each type also has its own pulse animation along the conduit, which is how you tell them apart at a glance. See Pulses and flow.

    The five types

    Every type is measured in the same unit, mana. Numbers in parentheses below are mana per day: what a source produces, or what a customer needs.

    Arcana

    Violet

    Raw magic, drawn from ley lines. Arcana is in the most demand of any type, and it's the only one that can be transformed into every other type.

    • Sources: Ley Line Nexus (30).
    • Needed by: Wizard Academy (10), Market Town (5).
    • Reacts with: Fire as Enchanted Flames; Water as Purification. No reaction with Earth or Gas.
    • Transforms into: Fire, Water, Earth or Gas, all at 3 : 1.
    • Made from: Water (2 : 1) or Gas (3 : 1).

    Fire

    Red-orange

    Heat for forges, academies and markets. Fire reacts with every other type, so it's the type most likely to cause trouble when routed carelessly.

    • Sources: Fire Geyser (30), Dragon Lair (30, when fed), Caldera and Flare Pit (15 each).
    • Needed by: Dwarven Forge (8), Wizard Academy (3), Goblin Bazaar (2).
    • Reacts with: Arcana as Enchanted Flames; Earth as Magma Vents; Gas as Explosion Risk; Water as Steam Clouds.
    • Transforms into: Gas (1.5 : 1) or Earth (3 : 1).
    • Made from: Gas (1.5 : 1), Earth (3 : 1) or Arcana (3 : 1).

    Water

    Blue

    For farms, mushrooms, academies and thirsty dragons. Water is the hardest type to make if a map doesn't have it: Arcana is the only thing a Transformer can turn into Water.

    • Sources: Underground Spring (30), Mud Spring (15).
    • Needed by: Farming Village (5), Mushroom Colony (3), Wizard Academy (2), Dragon Lair (2).
    • Reacts with: Earth as Fertile Growth; Arcana as Purification; Gas as Mineral Springs; Fire as Steam Clouds.
    • Transforms into: Arcana (2 : 1) or Earth (1.5 : 1).
    • Made from: Arcana only (3 : 1).

    Earth

    Green

    Solid, slow, dependable. Earth is a small part of many recipes and the key ingredient in two of the best money-making interactions.

    • Sources: Earth Vein (30), Caldera and Mud Spring (15 each).
    • Needed by: Dwarven Forge (5), Farming Village (3), Mushroom Colony (2).
    • Reacts with: Water as Fertile Growth; Gas as Mineral Deposits; Fire as Magma Vents. No reaction with Arcana.
    • Transforms into: Gas (2 : 1) or Fire (3 : 1).
    • Made from: Water (1.5 : 1), Fire (3 : 1) or Arcana (3 : 1).

    Gas

    Yellow-green

    Fuel for bazaar stalls and town lamps. Plenty of maps have no Gas Pocket at all, so gas often has to come from a hybrid or a Transformer.

    • Sources: Gas Pocket (30), Dwarven Forge and Mushroom Colony (12 each, when fed), Flare Pit (15).
    • Needed by: Goblin Bazaar (4), Market Town (3).
    • Reacts with: Earth as Mineral Deposits; Water as Mineral Springs; Fire as Explosion Risk. No reaction with Arcana.
    • Transforms into: Fire (1.5 : 1) or Arcana (3 : 1).
    • Made from: Fire (1.5 : 1), Earth (2 : 1) or Arcana (3 : 1).

    Demand figures above are each building's standard needs. Individual operations sometimes change them, so always check the building's tooltip in-game.

    Pulses and flow

    Mana travels as glowing pulses, and each type has its own look. The in-game Field Manual lists these figures, but they only describe the animation. They don't change how much mana is delivered.

    TypePulse rateFlow speedPulse sizePulse shape
    Arcana2.5/sec3.0 hex/sec5shimmering orb
    Fire4.0/sec4.0 hex/sec4flickering cluster
    Water1.5/sec2.0 hex/sec6stretched droplet
    Earth1.0/sec1.5 hex/sec7square block
    Gas2.0/sec2.5 hex/sec5scattered dots

    A conduit running above 80% of its capacity pulses faster and glows brighter as a warning that it's nearly full.

    Colorblind mode

    Turn on Colorblind Mode in Settings for a palette that avoids red/green confusion:

    • Arcana stays purple and Water stays blue.
    • Fire becomes orange, Earth becomes brown, and Gas becomes bright yellow.
    • The Content ring on customers turns blue instead of green.
